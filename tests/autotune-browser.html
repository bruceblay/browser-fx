Automatic note tuning with the new pitch engine, using the same effect as the extension. Open this page and YouTube in Chrome.

  1. Find a vocal on YouTube and start playing it.
  2. Click Connect YouTube tab, select that tab, and enable Share tab audio.
  3. Adjust the controls here, or switch between Original and Auto Tune while it plays.

A solo voice makes the correction easiest to hear. You can use any audio tab; full songs also shift the accompaniment.
